Getting Started with Business Ontology

Private Preview — Business Ontology must be enabled on your Snowflake account before use. Contact your Snowflake account team or SE.

What is Business Ontology?

Business Ontology is Snowflake's governed vocabulary layer. It lets you define what business concepts mean — metrics, entities, decoder tables, formulas — and how they relate to each other. Once defined, Cortex Agents use this knowledge to answer questions that require understanding beyond raw table schemas.

The core problem it solves: A Semantic View teaches an agent how to query (tables, columns, joins). Business Ontology teaches it what the answer means (which vendors count as "canonical", how to handle credit memos, what formula to use for COGS).

Semantic View Alone

"How many suppliers do we have?"
COUNT(*) FROM LFA1
15
Wrong — includes probationary (ZPRB) vendor

Semantic View + Business Ontology

"How many suppliers do we have?"
COUNT(DISTINCT LIFNR) FROM LFA1
WHERE KTOKK IN ('ZSTR','ZSTD')
14
Correct — excludes probationary per business policy

Phase 1: Create Domains

$business-ontology Create a domain called SAP Purchasing
$business-ontology Create a domain called SAP Finance
$business-ontology Create a domain called SAP Sales

Behind the scenes: The skill calls SYSTEM$CREATE_GLOSSARY_DOMAIN(). No draft/approve needed — domains are created immediately.

Phase 2: Add Entities (11 total)

Entities are business objects. They have descriptions but no formulas.

SAP Purchasing — 7 entities

$business-ontology Add an entity called Supplier to SAP Purchasing. Description: Vendor master (LFA1). KTOKK: ZSTR=Strategic, ZSTD=Standard, ZPRB=Probationary. Exclude ZPRB from canonical counts.
$business-ontology Add an entity called Material to SAP Purchasing. Description: Material master (MARA). MATKL: 043=Electronics, 044=Chemicals, 045=Metals, 046=Packaging, 047=Raw Materials. STPRS=standard price USD.
$business-ontology Add an entity called Purchase Order to SAP Purchasing. Description: PO items (EKPO). STATU: O=Open, C=Closed, R=Received. NETWR=net value, MENGE=quantity.
$business-ontology Add an entity called Shipment to SAP Purchasing. Description: Deliveries (LIKP). Compare WADAT vs LFDAT for OTD. Do NOT use LFA2.OTRAT (unreliable).
$business-ontology Add an entity called Carrier to SAP Purchasing. Description: Forwarding agents (LFA2). VSART: 01=Ocean, 02=FTL, 03=Air, 04=Intermodal, 05=LTL. OTRAT is self-reported — unreliable.
$business-ontology Add an entity called Inspection to SAP Purchasing. Description: Quality inspection lots (QALS). VCODE: A=Accepted, R=Rejected, C=Conditional. QAESSION=defect rate.
$business-ontology Add an entity called BOM Item to SAP Purchasing. Description: Bill of Materials (STPO). STLNR=parent, IDNRK=child, MENGE=qty. 4-level recursive hierarchy.

SAP Finance — 2 entities

$business-ontology Add an entity called AP Line Item to SAP Finance. Description: Accounting lines (BSEG). BSCHL: 31=Invoice (POSITIVE), 34=Credit memo (SUBTRACT). HKONT=GL account.
$business-ontology Add an entity called Cost Posting to SAP Finance. Description: Cost postings (COEP). OBJNR: KS-MFG-US/EU/AP=Manufacturing, KS-LOG=Logistics, KS-QA=Quality.

SAP Sales — 2 entities

$business-ontology Add an entity called Sales Order to SAP Sales. Description: Sales items (VBAP). VKORG: 1000=Americas, 2000=EMEA, 3000=APAC. NETWR=net value.
$business-ontology Add an entity called Pricing Condition to SAP Sales. Description: Pricing conditions (KONV). KSCHL: PR00=Base, K007=Discount (REDUCES revenue), KF00=Surcharge (ADDS).

Phase 3: Add Metrics with Formulas (11 total)

Metrics include a formula field — the key differentiator from Semantic View metrics.

SAP Purchasing — 6 metrics

$business-ontology Add a metric called Canonical Supplier Count to SAP Purchasing. Formula: COUNT(DISTINCT LIFNR) FROM LFA1 WHERE KTOKK IN ('ZSTR','ZSTD') Description: Excludes ZPRB probationary. Answer is 14, not 15.
$business-ontology Add a metric called Operational OTD Rate to SAP Purchasing. Formula: COUNT(CASE WHEN WADAT <= LFDAT THEN 1 END) / COUNT(*) FROM LIKP WHERE STATU='D' Description: Real OTD ~66.7%. WARNING: Do NOT use LFA2.OTRAT (~90%, unreliable).
$business-ontology Add a metric called Total Procurement Spend to SAP Purchasing. Formula: SUM(NETWR) FROM EKPO Description: Use EKPO.NETWR only. NOT LFB1.JWERT (contract values, much larger).
$business-ontology Add a metric called Weighted Supply Risk Score to SAP Purchasing. Formula: 0.4 * single_source_flag + 0.3 * defect_rate + 0.3 * delivery_failure_rate Description: Fixed policy weights. Top: MAT-006 at 0.70.
$business-ontology Add a metric called Supplier Disruption Cascade to SAP Purchasing. Formula: Recursive: Supplier -> EKPO(materials) -> STPO(recursive BOM upward) -> affected assemblies Description: 5-hop cross-domain traversal from supplier to revenue impact.
$business-ontology Add a metric called BOM Cost Rollup to SAP Purchasing. Formula: Recursive CTE on STPO downward, multiply MENGE, JOIN MARA.STPRS for leaf costs Description: For ASSY-004: $286.60/unit. Do NOT use assembly STPRS ($520).

SAP Finance — 3 metrics

$business-ontology Add a metric called COGS Raw Materials to SAP Finance. Formula: SUM(CASE WHEN BSCHL='31' THEN DMBTR WHEN BSCHL='34' THEN -DMBTR END) FROM BSEG WHERE HKONT='0040100000' Description: CRITICAL: Credit memos (34) SUBTRACT. Correct: $1,290,020. Wrong: $1,353,690.
$business-ontology Add a metric called Product Gross Margin to SAP Finance. Formula: VBAP.NETWR - (BOM_Cost_Rollup * VBAP.KWMENG) Description: Cross-domain: Sales revenue minus BOM cost. No direct FK — chain goes through BOM.
$business-ontology Add a metric called Cost Center Total to SAP Finance. Formula: SUM(WRTBTR) FROM COEP WHERE OBJNR = cost_object_code Description: KS-MFG-US=US Manufacturing ($419,750), KS-LOG=Logistics, KS-QA=Quality.

SAP Sales — 2 metrics

$business-ontology Add a metric called Discount Leakage to SAP Sales. Formula: SUM(ABS(KWERT)) FROM KONV WHERE KSCHL='K007' Description: K007=discount only. EXCLUDE KF00 (surcharges) and PR00 (base prices).
$business-ontology Add a metric called Disruption Revenue Impact to SAP Sales. Formula: SUM(VBAP.NETWR) for assemblies from Supplier Disruption Cascade Description: Revenue at risk. 5-hop: Supplier -> Materials -> BOM -> Assemblies -> Sales.

Surfacing Ontology Through a Cortex Agent

The glossary is the single source of truth. The SP reads from it dynamically — no hardcoded business logic.
